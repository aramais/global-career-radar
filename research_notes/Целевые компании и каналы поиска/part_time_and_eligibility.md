## 1. Do Brazilian bigtechs, LATAM tech and global remote companies offer part-time or reduced-hours roles at manager/lead level?

### Takeaway
I found no published part-time or reduced-hours policy for manager/lead roles at Nubank, iFood, Mercado Libre, QuintoAndar, PicPay, Stone, GitLab, Automattic, Shopify or Deel, and no published phased-return-after-parental-leave program at any of them. In Brazil, reduced-hours arrangements show up as small pilots (4-day week), split parental leaves or individual deals. For a senior analytics leader, the realistic reduced-hours routes are a negotiated individual arrangement or fractional/contract work (section 3), not a catalogued "part-time Head of Analytics" job.

The same article also names Grupo Boticário (120 days, equal for fathers and mothers). It does not mention iFood, Mercado Livre, QuintoAndar, PicPay, Stone or Google. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Forbes Brasil, 2026-03-31](https://forbes.com.br/carreira/2026/03/ampliacao-da-licenca-paternidade-e-aprovada-entenda-os-beneficios/)

### Inferences
- `HYPOTHESIS`: at the named bigtechs, manager/lead analytics roles are effectively full-time. If reduced hours happen, they are probably individual deals for people already inside the company with a track record, not offers to external hires. Getting one at entry is unlikely; getting one after 6–12 months is more plausible.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: generous parental leave is the main "family flex" lever these companies publish. Nubank's 120 days for fathers is far above the statutory 5 or 20 days. That points to a strategy of aiming for full-time roles with strong leave and remote/async norms rather than part-time roles.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: global remote companies (GitLab, Automattic) offer schedule autonomy and async work, which can feel like reduced load. Contractually, though, they hire full-time. When they employ through an entity or EOR in Brazil, Brazilian CLT rules apply, including section 2's part-time rules.

### Gaps
- No published policy or dated evidence was found for iFood, QuintoAndar, PicPay or Stone parental leave or part-time. A 2021 list naming QuintoAndar among companies with above-statutory paternity leave surfaced in search, but I could not pin it to a confirmed URL or number of days.
- No data found on the share of part-time or reduced-hours roles at manager level in Brazilian tech (CAGED data not checked). Glassdoor benefits pages were not reachable or tested.
- Shopify and Deel part-time policies were not found (not searched in depth). Shopify's and Deel's own parental-leave pages were not checked.
- Whether Nubank's 120 days needs a minimum tenure, or covers employees still in the contrato de experiência, was not found. This matters for a start date close to the birth.

## 2. Brazil legal forms: CLT part-time, PJ/pejotização, MEI, contractor/EOR for foreign employers

### Takeaway
CLT part-time (art. 58-A, as amended by Lei 13.467/2017) allows up to 30 h/week without overtime, or up to 26 h/week with up to 6 overtime hours. It exists but is rare at leadership level. PJ contracting is the common flexible form in tech. The STF's general-repercussion case on pejotização (Tema 1389) was still pending in 2026, so reclassification risk is legally unsettled. MEI does not fit analytics consulting. Foreign employers either hire through an EOR (Brazilian CLT, so the worker must be able to work in Brazil) or pay a Brazilian contractor, usually PJ with a CNPJ.

### Cited Findings
- **CLT art. 58-A after the 2017 reform:** part-time means up to 30 hours/week with no overtime, or up to 26 hours/week with up to 6 overtime hours/week paid at +50%. Before the reform the limit was 25 h/week with no overtime. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` (secondary summaries of the statute) — [Findes Informe Estratégico, 2025-06](https://findes.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Informe-Estrategico-–-Regime-de-Tempo-Parcial-Conceito-Regras-e-Aplicacoes.pdf); [Jus.com.br](https://jus.com.br/artigos/63554/reforma-trabalhista-como-fica-o-trabalho-em-regime-de-tempo-parcial); official text to check: [CLT, Planalto](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/decreto-lei/del5452.htm) `TO_VERIFY` (Planalto not reachable during research)
- **Holidays and conversion:** after the reform, part-time employees follow the general holiday rules and may sell part of their holidays (abono). Sources disagree on proportionality (art. 58-A §7 refers to CLT art. 130). For existing employees, switching to part-time is valid only through collective negotiation. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Jus.com.br](https://jus.com.br/artigos/63554/reforma-trabalhista-como-fica-o-trabalho-em-regime-de-tempo-parcial); [ABMES comparison table](https://Abmes.Org.Br/arquivos/documentos/reforma_trabalhista_quadro_comparativo.pdf)
- **Pejotização, Tema 1389 (STF, ARE 1.532.603, rapporteur Gilmar Mendes):** general repercussion was recognized in April 2025, and on 2025-04-14 Mendes ordered a nationwide suspension of cases on the lawfulness of hiring autonomous workers or PJs. The case also covers Labour Court jurisdiction and the burden of proof for fraud. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Movimento Econômico, 2025-04-14](https://movimentoeconomico.com.br/economia/justica/2025/04/14/pejotizacao-stf-suspende-todas-as-acoes-de-empresas-e-trabalhadores/); [ANAMATRA technical note 2025](https://novo.anamatra.org.br/images/DOCUMENTOS/2025/NOTA_TECNICA.PEJOTIZACAO.pdf)
- **Tema 1389 status in 2026:** a March 2026 article describes the judgment as still in progress. Search snippets report a Cármen Lúcia request for review time (vista) in December 2025 and a February 2026 PGR opinion favouring the constitutionality of PJ hiring. An August 2026 Findes brief reports Mendes suspending enforcement of a labour judgment on pejotização pending Tema 1389. No final merits ruling was found as of 2026-10-15. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` / `TO_VERIFY` (check the STF case record) — [Contabilidade.com, 2026](https://contabilidade.com/blog/pejotizacao-no-stf-pode-mudar-tudo-em-2026-entenda-os-riscos-para-empresas-e-contadores/); [Findes, 2026-08](https://findes.com.br/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Informe-Estrategico-–-STF-suspende-execucao-de-condenacao-trabalhista-por-pejotizacao.pdf)
- **Classification factors and penalties (Deel, updated 2026-06-09):** the factors are control/supervision, personal performance, economic dependence, integration into the organization, and regularity/continuity. Reclassification exposure includes retroactive wages and benefits, severance, FGTS, social-security contributions, fines and collective damages. Fixed schedules and exclusivity are red flags. Deel recommends an EOR where the risk is high. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` (vendor) — [Deel: Brazil misclassification](https://www.deel.com/blog/brazil-employee-misclassification-risks-and-best-practices/)
- **A CNPJ does not settle classification:** a 2024 report says multinationals in Brazil asked some new CLT hires to close their CNPJs to secure exclusivity. Whether that practice is lawful is unclear. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Baguete, 2024](https://www.baguete.com.br/noticias/empresas-pedem-baixa-do-cnpj-de-novos-contratados)
- **MEI does not fit consulting or intellectual work:** consultoria is not on the Portal do Empreendedor's list of permitted MEI activities, so a consultant "cannot be MEI". The suggested alternatives are Empresário Individual or a Sociedade Limitada Unipessoal. Intellectual, scientific or technical activities requiring specialized training are generally excluded. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Jornal Contábil](https://www.jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/consultor-pode-ser-mei-confira-os-detalhes/); [Contábeis, 2025](https://www.contabeis.com.br/noticias/69055/mei-quais-sao-as-ocupacoes-permitidas-em-2025/). The official list on gov.br/Portal do Empreendedor was not checked line by line: `TO_VERIFY`.
- **Foreigners and MEI:** possible if the person is legally regularized. Secondary sources say the accepted migration documents are the CRNM, the Documento Provisório de RNM, or a refugee-request protocol. Older sources also mention "visto permanente". `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` (dated 2018–2023) — [Remessa Online](https://www.remessaonline.com.br/blog/mei-para-estrangeiros/); [Jornal Contábil](https://jornalcontabil.com.br/noticia/sou-estrangeiro-posso-ser-mei/)
- **EOR vs contractor for foreign employers:** a foreign company without a Brazilian entity cannot sponsor a work authorization. Employer-sponsored work authorization goes through the Ministry of Labour and then a VITEM V visa, so it does not apply to a person already on another residence track. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Baker McKenzie resource hub](https://resourcehub.bakermckenzie.com/en/resources/global-immigration-and-mobility/latin-america/brazil/topics/employment-assignments); [RemotePass (vendor)](https://www.remotepass.com/country/brazil/work-visas)
- **Brazilian invoicing basics for contractors:** a Brazilian issuer needs a CNPJ, and service invoices are electronic NFS-e. A dual CBS/IBS VAT starts phasing in from 2026. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` (weak, generic invoice-generator source) — [ohmyfin](https://ohmyfin.ai/tr/invoice/requirements/brazil)

### Inferences
- `HYPOTHESIS`: for a senior analytics leader, the workable reduced-hours forms are:
  - (a) CLT full-time with a negotiated individual schedule. Leadership roles are often "cargo de confiança", excluded from hour controls under CLT art. 62 II (`TO_VERIFY`).
  - (b) CLT part-time under art. 58-A. Legal, but the pay is proportional and it is rarely offered for leadership roles.
  - (c) PJ (ME/SLU under Simples Nacional, not MEI) serving several clients. Multiple clients, deliverable-based scope and no fixed schedule lower the pejotização risk.
  - (d) A foreign client paying a Brazilian PJ for exported services.
- `HYPOTHESIS`: a part-time, multi-client, scope-based fractional engagement is structurally less exposed to "vínculo empregatício" than a single-client, full-time "PJ de crachá". Tema 1389 is still pending, though, so the legal standard may change.
- `TO_VERIFY` (tax, belongs to the migration/tax knowledge base): Simples Nacional annex and "fator R" for analytics/consulting CNAEs, the tax treatment of service exports, and how PJ income interacts with Brazilian tax residency and the current Russian employment.

## 3. Fractional/interim market: demand, platforms, engagement models, rates, positioning

### Takeaway
The fractional market is real and mostly remote. Go Fractional's June 2026 data shows an average rate of about $153/h and featured C-suite engagements of about 20–27 h/week. Data/analytics is a small share of postings (3%) but under-supplied (demand/supply ratio 1.7×). Fractional leaders get work mainly through their networks (92%); platforms are secondary (29%). There is no mature Brazil-specific fractional analytics-leadership marketplace. US/EU platforms and personal networks are the main channels, and LATAM is positioned as a cheaper source of talent.

### Cited Findings
- **Go Fractional "2026 State of Fractional Work" (PDF dated 2026-06-09):** based on Fractional Job Board data (15,000+ talent profiles, 1,000+ live jobs) plus a survey of 213 fractional professionals.
  - Average fractional rate: $153/h.
  - 72% of tracked roles were trending up versus the previous 90 days.
  - About half of respondents work with 2–3 clients at once.
  - 92% rely on personal networks as their main channel; 29% use fractional talent platforms.
  - 90% name client acquisition as their biggest challenge.
  - Remote is the default model.
  - The best relationships are retainer-based, billed upfront and 3+ months long.
  - Hourly pricing dominates; outcome-based deals are the exception.
  `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` (marketplace data, not an audited survey) — [Go Fractional 2026 report](https://www.gofractional.com/docs/2026_State_of_Fractional_Work-Go_Fractional.pdf)

- `HYPOTHESIS`, rate band for a senior analytics leader:

- `HYPOTHESIS`, positioning:
  - Go Fractional's data says the winners are "builders" with a 2–3 client portfolio, network-driven sourcing and 3+ month retainers.
  - Lead with P&L-linked outcomes (pricing, unit economics, marketplace/real-estate analytics, experimentation), not generic dashboards.
  - Package offers as fixed-scope diagnostics (30/60/90-day plans) that convert into retainers.

- `HYPOTHESIS`: fractional work fits a newborn period better than a new full-time job: hours can be controlled, there is no probation and it is multi-client. ## 4. Parental leave in Brazil as of 2026 and how a job change near a late-November-2026 birth interacts with it

### Takeaway
For a birth in late November 2026, statutory paternity leave is still **5 days**, or **20 days** at an Empresa Cidadã company. The new law that phases it up to 10/15/20 days (and adds job stability and an INSS salário-paternidade, including for MEIs) only takes effect on **2027-01-01**. Company policies matter much more: Nubank gives fathers 120 days, but tenure eligibility is unknown. A PJ/contractor has no statutory paternity leave in 2026.

### Cited Findings
- **New law sanctioned 2026-03-31 (reported as Lei 15.371/2026):** the schedule is 10 days from 2027-01-01, 15 days in 2028 and 20 days in 2029. The law creates a salário-paternidade (employer pays and INSS reimburses) that also covers MEIs, domestic workers, avulsos, contribuintes individuais and segurados especiais. It protects employment from the start of the leave until one month after it ends; if a dismissal prevents the leave, the employer pays double. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Migalhas, 2026-03-31](https://www.migalhas.com.br/quentes/453008/lula-sanciona-lei-que-amplia-licenca-paternidade-de-5-para-20-dias); [Arpen-SP](https://arpensp.org.br/noticia/governo-sanciona-lei-que-amplia-licenca-paternidade-para-20-dias-e-cria-salario-paternidade); [IBDFAM](https://ibdfam.org.br/noticias/13759/Sancionada+lei+que+amplia+licen%C3%A7a-paternidade+e+cria+sal%C3%A1rio-paternidade)
- **In 2026 the leave stays at 5 days.** Arpen-SP adds that stability runs from the employee's notice to the employer until one month after the leave, and that the leave can be extended if mother or baby is hospitalized. `PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL` — [Congresso em Foco](https://www.congressoemfoco.com.br/noticia/117746/lula-sanciona-lei-que-aumenta-a-licenca-paternidade-para-20-dias); [Arpen-SP](https://arpensp.org.br/noticia/governo-sanciona-lei-que-amplia-licenca-paternidade-para-20-dias-e-cria-salario-paternidade)
- **Conflicts to flag:**
  - The norm number: most sources say Lei 15.371/2026, but Congresso em Foco and Wagner Advogados reportedly say "Lei Complementar 229/2026".

## 5. Employment-eligibility prerequisites and the candidate's current migration status

### Takeaway
On a visitor (tourist) stay, Lei 13.445 art. 13 §1 bars "exercer atividade remunerada no Brasil". That likely blocks CLT employment or PJ work for Brazilian payers until the residence process starts. Remote work for a foreign employer is treated in the canon as permissible, but that is `TO_VERIFY`. Once the family-reunion (parent-of-Brazilian) residence is filed and the PF protocol issued (expected ~December 2026, after the birth), the person gets the rights of the Migration Law for up to 180 days. Under the current labour portaria, a PF protocol is listed among the documents accepted for a CTPS, but a revoked 2018 rule had excluded family-reunion protocols. Whether a protocol is enough for CLT hiring before the CRNM (expected ~April–August 2027) is the key `TO_VERIFY`.
