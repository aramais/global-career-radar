# Global remote-first / remote-friendly companies hiring analytics leaders based in Brazil (snapshot 2026-10-15)

Scope: global companies not founded in LATAM that hire analytics or decision-science leaders remotely from Brazil, or under "LATAM / Americas / anywhere". Brazil roles tied to a São Paulo office are left to the other researcher and only flagged here. LATAM-founded firms are excluded (Stone, QuintoAndar, VTEX, Wildlife, Gympass/Wellhub, EBANX, CI&T, among others).

Method and confidence labels:
- **ATS snapshot.** On 2026-10-15 I pulled the public job-board APIs: Greenhouse `boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{org}/jobs`, Ashby `api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{org}`, Lever `api.lever.co/v0/postings/{org}`, and Atlassian's listings endpoint. This covered about 100 company boards. "Live" below means the posting was in the employer's own ATS feed on 2026-10-15. Each posting URL is the employer's own page.
- **Labels.** VERIFIED_OFFICIAL means an employer page, posting or handbook. PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL means press, aggregators or Glassdoor. HYPOTHESIS means my own inference. TO_VERIFY means it needs a direct check.
- **Search budget.** The shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through the session. Some culture and layoff checks could not be finished; they are listed under Gaps.

## Which global remote-first companies explicitly hire in Brazil (or "LATAM / Americas / anywhere")?

### Takeaway
Few of the well-known remote-first brands hire into Brazil for analytics leadership. The best live fits as of 2026-10-15:
- **Customer.io**: Senior Manager, Data Science & Analytics, "Americas Remote".
- **Kraken**: Brazil is listed on 38 of 87 open roles, including Principal, Business Operations – Pro.

Wikimedia, Coinbase, GitLab, Twilio, Canonical, Clipboard Health, Hopper, Supabase, Toptal and Elastic do hire in Brazil or under "Americas/Global", but right now only into IC, sales or engineering roles, or into analytics leadership with a different scope. Zapier is explicitly excluded: its own country list says Brazil is "no under Zapier policy". Atlassian, Stripe, PostHog, Docker, Vercel, Dropbox, Okta, Mozilla and Hostinger posted no Brazil-eligible roles.

## How do these companies employ Brazil residents, and what changed in 2025–2026 (RTO, narrowing countries, layoffs)?

### Takeaway
Brazil hires at global remote companies mostly go through an EOR:
- **Confirmed EOR**: Wikimedia (third-party EOR) and GitLab (Remote.com entity "Remote Technology").
- **Local payroll in BRL, mechanism unconfirmed**: Coinbase.
