## 1. Finding people: LinkedIn search, Sales Navigator vs Recruiter Lite, X-ray, and mapping an analytics org

### Takeaway
LinkedIn people search (Boolean operators plus company, past-company, school and location filters) is still the main tool. Google X-ray has lost much of its value since LinkedIn cut Google's indexing of profile fields in January 2024. Conference speaker pages, community organizers, company tech blogs, podcasts and job postings are the best sources of named, contextual targets. The strongest causal evidence (Science, 2022) says moderately weak ties, the people you share some mutual connections with, produce the most job mobility, especially in digital industries.

### Cited Findings
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, 2026] LinkedIn keyword search supports exact-phrase quotes, AND, OR, NOT and parentheses. Operators must be typed in capitals; lowercase "and" or "not" are treated as ordinary words. Boolean works in standard search, Recruiter and Sales Navigator, but field prefixes such as `title:` reportedly do not work in the free search bar, which matches against the whole public profile, including recommendations. — [100hires, "LinkedIn Boolean search… 2026"](https://100hires.com/linkedin-boolean-search.html). LinkedIn's own help article ("Use Boolean search on LinkedIn", answer a524335) is cited there but was not retrieved. **TO_VERIFY** on LinkedIn Help.
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL / TO_VERIFY] Free accounts hit a monthly "commercial use limit" on search when activity looks like recruiting or prospecting. It resets on the 1st of the month and the threshold is unpublished (people report figures of 60 or 100). After that, only a handful of results show. — [University of Toronto Mississauga careers guide](https://utm.utoronto.ca/careers/media/5127/download); [ERE](https://www.ere.net/articles/new-linkedin-rule-search-for-free-until-you-cant); [SocialTalent](https://www.socialtalent.com/?p=16358). One source says Premium plans are not subject to it ([Habr](https://habr.com/en/articles/466801)). **TO_VERIFY.**
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, 2026] Sales Navigator has about 29 filters, Recruiter Lite about 21. Filters only in Sales Navigator: years in current company, years in current role, company type, groups, HQ location. Filters only in Recruiter Lite: graduation year and skills. One 2026 buyer's guide frames the choice as role-centred (Recruiter Lite) vs account-centred (Sales Navigator). — [100hires, Recruiter Lite vs Sales Navigator 2026](https://100hires.com/linkedin-recruiter-lite-vs-sales-navigator.html)
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, 2026 / TO_VERIFY] Monthly InMail credits by plan: Premium Career 5, Premium Business 15, Sales Navigator 50 (same across Core, Advanced and Advanced Plus), Recruiter Lite 30. Messages to Premium "Open Profiles" use no credit. Credits are refunded when the recipient replies, even with a no. Listed prices: Premium Career about $29.99–39.99/month, Sales Navigator Core about $99.99–119.99/month. — [Zeliq 2026](https://www.zeliq.com/blog/linkedin-inmail); [SalesRobot](https://www.salesrobot.co/blogs/linkedin-inmail-credits); [SalesBread 2026](https://salesbread.com/how-much-does-linkedin-premium-cost/). - [VERIFIED_OFFICIAL, peer-reviewed, 2022] "A Causal Test of the Strength of Weak Ties" (Rajkumar, Saint-Jacques, Bojinov, Brynjolfsson, Aral; *Science*, 15 Sep 2022). The authors ran randomized experiments on LinkedIn's People You May Know feature covering more than 20M members over 5 years (2B new ties, 600k new jobs). Tie strength had an inverted-U relationship with job transmission. Moderately weak ties (measured by mutual connections) and the weakest ties (measured by interaction intensity) produced the most job mobility. Weak ties helped more in digital industries; strong ties helped more in less digital ones. — [Stanford Digital Economy Lab summary](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/publication/a-causal-test-of-the-strength-of-weak-ties); [Science News](https://sciencenews.org/?p=3117214); [MIT IDE](https://ide.mit.edu/insights/new-publication-mit-harvard-and-stanford-scientists-show-weaker-ties-are-more-beneficial-for-job-seekers-on-linkedin/)
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, 2016, dated] Earlier LinkedIn economist data: only about 11–12% of job switchers had a first-degree connection at their new company six months before the move. Second- and third-degree ties mattered more. — [Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/3060887/what-linkedin-data-reveals-about-who-will-help-you-get-your-next-job)

### Inferences
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Title Boolean strings** (paste into the Title filter, or into keywords with location set to "São Paulo, Brazil" / "Brazil" / "Latin America"):
  - Leaders: `("head of analytics" OR "head of data" OR "director of analytics" OR "director of data" OR "analytics director" OR "VP data" OR "VP analytics" OR "chief data officer" OR CDO OR CDAO OR "head de dados" OR "diretor de dados" OR "diretora de dados" OR "head de analytics" OR "superintendente de dados") NOT (intern OR estagiário OR consultant)`
  - Hiring-manager band: `("analytics manager" OR "gerente de analytics" OR "gerente de dados" OR "gerente de BI" OR "coordenador de dados" OR "data science manager" OR "product analytics lead" OR "growth analytics")`
  - Recruiters: `("tech recruiter" OR "talent acquisition" OR "recrutador" OR "tech recruiting" OR "talent partner") AND (data OR dados OR analytics)`
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Filter stacks worth running:** (a) Current company = target, plus title string, plus 2nd-degree only, to find warm paths. (b) Past company = Yandex / CIAN / other ex-CIS tech, with location = Brazil or LATAM, for alumni at target companies. (c) School = Lomonosov Moscow State University, with location = Brazil / Latin America / "remote" companies. (d) Current company = target, plus "Portuguese"/"Russian" in profile language, to find bilingual bridges. Each alumnus is a "moderately weak tie", the most productive band in the Science study.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Map one company's analytics org in about 30 minutes:** (1) list the top data leader (CDO, Head of Data) from LinkedIn and Gartner or dbt speaker pages. (2) Pull current job postings and note team names (Credit, Growth, Marketplace, Risk, Product Analytics) and the "reports to" lines. (3) Search LinkedIn for "[team] analytics" + company to find managers per team. (4) Find blog or Medium authors and podcast guests (Data Hackers, company blogs) and tag who writes publicly, since they are the most approachable. (5) Pick 2–3 people per company at different levels (one peer or IC, one manager, one recruiter) rather than one senior target.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Sales Navigator vs Recruiter Lite for a job seeker:** Sales Navigator suits the "account map" workflow (company lists, tenure filters, HQ filter, saved lead alerts when people change jobs or post). Recruiter Lite's distinctive filters (graduation year, skills) are less useful for finding leaders. A single month of Premium Career is the cheapest way to lift the 5-notes-per-month cap (see §6) and get 5 InMails. Only buy Sales Navigator if a 30–40-company target list needs systematic mapping; one month aligned with an outreach sprint is enough.

## 2. Communities: Brazil/LATAM, global analytics, ex-CIS diaspora and alumni, and how to engage without spamming

### Takeaway
For Brazil, the broadest data community is Data Hackers (newsletter, podcast, survey; current Slack status unclear). Mulheres em Dados (Discord, about 13k members) is a women's space, and local meetups (R-Ladies SP, dbt meetups, ODSC) are smaller but more personal. The global communities with the most senior members are Locally Optimistic (leaders; strict no-solicitation rule), Lenny's paid Slack (30k+, product and growth), dbt Slack (80k–100k+), Measure Slack (15k–25k, digital analytics) and Experiment Nation (experimentation). The Russian-speaking diaspora in Brazil is documented academically, but I could not verify any specific active Telegram or Facebook groups. A Yandex alumni community (YNDX Family via AlumniHub) exists.

### Inferences
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Priority by decision-maker density:** (1) Locally Optimistic (leaders; apply with a thoughtful intro, contribute in public threads, never cold-DM to ask for jobs). (2) Lenny's paid Slack (product and growth leaders at global remote companies; $150/year is cheap for 2–3 months of targeted activity). (3) Data Hackers channels (Brazilian reach; podcast and newsletter for target names; post a guest article or survey insight). (4) dbt Slack local channels and dbt meetups in São Paulo (for analytics-engineering managers). (5) Measure Slack and Experiment Nation (marketing, product analytics and experimentation niches). Mulheres em Dados is a women's network; respect its membership criteria.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Engagement pattern that avoids spam:** introduce yourself once in #intros. Spend 2–4 weeks answering questions where marketplace, classifieds, pricing or experimentation experience is rare, and share one useful write-up (for example, a "pricing experiments in classifieds" teardown). Then ask in public for a "20-min chat with anyone running analytics at X", and DM only people who reply or react. This follows Locally Optimistic's explicit no-solicitation norm.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Diaspora:** the most practical route is LinkedIn filters (past company Yandex / Mail.ru / VK / Avito / CIAN; school MSU; location Brazil or LATAM). Next is asking YNDX Family / AlumniHub contacts for introductions, then in-person diaspora events (Russian House, SPbU representation) as social rather than professional channels. Telegram groups must be found manually inside Telegram; vet them for scams and politics before posting.

## 4. Message design: templates, follow-up cadence, response-rate evidence, English vs Portuguese

### Takeaway
The best large-sample evidence (LinkedIn, May 2022, tens of millions of recruiter InMails) shows that short messages under 400 characters do 22% better than average, individually written ones 15% better, and that 90% of replies arrive within a week. For LinkedIn invites, a 2025 dataset of 112k sequences found a note slightly lowers acceptance but raises replies among those who accept (8.18% vs 5.28%). Job-seeker outreach should follow the Dalton pattern: under 100 words, no job ask in the first message, connection first. Referrals convert far better than applications (Ashby: 40% vs 3% application-to-interview). There is no evidence on English vs Portuguese openers. Brazil's English proficiency is "low" nationally but higher in São Paulo city (518 vs 482), so an English message with a one-line Portuguese greeting is a reasonable default (HYPOTHESIS).

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED_OFFICIAL, published 19 May 2022; data May 2021–Apr 2022; recruiter-to-candidate direction] InMails under 400 characters: +22% response vs average; up to 800: +5%; 800–1,200: −6%; over 1,200: −11%. Individually sent vs bulk: about +15%. "Open to Work" candidates: +37%. Friday −4%, Saturday −8%, Sunday–Thursday within about 1% of average. 65% of responses arrive within 24 hours and 90% within one week. Only 10% of InMails are under 400 characters. — [LinkedIn Talent Blog](https://www.linkedin.com/business/talent/blog/talent-strategy/these-inmails-get-best-response-rates). LinkedIn cautions that short messages are rare in the dataset.
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, Belkins 2026 study of 2025 data; sales context] LinkedIn, 112,005 invite-based sequences: an intro note lowered acceptance (25.31% vs 27.56%) but raised replies among accepted invites (8.18% vs 5.28%). ### Inferences

#### Message rules (HYPOTHESIS, built from the evidence above)
- Keep messages under 400 characters on LinkedIn and under 100 words by email. One specific hook (their talk, post, team launch or job posting), one line of credibility, one small, time-boxed ask (15–20 minutes, call or coffee), and an easy out.
- Lead with what you share: a mutual connection, the same community, an event, or alumni status (Yandex or MSU alumni are moderately weak ties).
- Do not ask for a job or referral in the first message (Dalton). Ask for perspective on a specific topic: how their analytics org is structured, how they run experiments, what "analytics close to P&L" means at their company.
- Personalize every message individually (+15% for individual vs bulk InMails). Avoid Friday afternoons and weekends (−4% / −8%).
- **Language:** write in English and open with a short Portuguese line such as "Olá, [Nome], tudo bem?". Add one honest sentence: "Sorry for writing in English — my Portuguese is still basic (estou aprendendo!)". São Paulo tech leaders at bigtechs and global companies are more likely than average to work in English (HYPOTHESIS, consistent with São Paulo city's above-national EF score). For recruiters at Brazilian companies, offer to continue in Portuguese by voice or with written help if needed.

#### Templates (HYPOTHESIS; adapt and never mass-send)
1. **LinkedIn connection note** (about 250 characters; free accounts get only 5 notes a month, see §6):
   > Olá, [Nome]! Sorry for the English — my Portuguese is still basic. I lead analytics in marketplaces/classifieds (ex-Yandex, CIAN) and recently moved to São Paulo. Your [talk/post] on [topic] was very useful. Would be glad to connect.
2. **Informational conversation** (after the invite is accepted, or by email; under 100 words):
   > Subject: Question about analytics at [Company] ([shared context])
   > Hi [Name], [we're both in Locally Optimistic / I enjoyed your Data Hackers episode on X]. I've led analytics teams at Yandex and CIAN (classifieds, e-commerce) and am now based in São Paulo, learning how data teams at Brazilian tech companies sit relative to the business and P&L. Your work on [specific] stood out. Would you be open to a 20-minute call or a coffee near [Faria Lima/Pinheiros] in the next few weeks? Happy to share what worked for us on [pricing experiments / marketplace metrics] in return.
3. **Follow-up** (about day 7, once):
   > Hi [Name], gently bumping this in case it got buried. Totally understand if now isn't a good time. Even a 2-line answer on [one specific question] would help a lot. Obrigado!
4. **Referral ask** (only after a conversation and only for a specific opening; make it easy to say no):
   > Thanks again for the chat on [date]. I saw [Company] opened [Role, link/ID]. Given what you said about [team priority], I think [1–2 concrete proof points] fit well. Would you be comfortable submitting an indicação? I've attached a 3-bullet summary and CV you can forward as-is. No worries at all if not — your advice was already very helpful.
   - Include a forwardable blurb of 3 bullets (scope, P&L-linked impact, why this team) so the referrer spends no effort and takes no reputational risk.
5. **Recruiter message** (direct is fine here): role ID or link, one-line fit, location (São Paulo, hybrid or remote), language ("working language English; Portuguese in progress"), availability, and "Can I share my CV for this or similar manager roles?"
6. **Post-event WhatsApp** (only to numbers given in person):
   > Oi [Nome]! Foi ótimo te conhecer hoje no [evento]. Vamos marcar aquele café? (Happy to continue in English.)

#### Cadence and volume (HYPOTHESIS)
- Day 0 first message. Day 5–7 one follow-up (90% of InMail replies arrive within a week). Day 14–21 close the loop, or move to a second person at the same company. Stop after 2 follow-ups. Sales data (Belkins) favours longer sequences, but personal networking has higher reputational cost.
- Volume: about 10–20 highly personalized new contacts a week across 5–8 target companies, far below the unpublished invitation cap (see §6). Track conversations in the HR-Automation CRM (single canon for statuses and next actions per the project's AGENTS.md) with minimal fields (see §6).
- Voice notes: optional, only for 1st-degree connections who have already engaged, under 45–60 seconds, never as the opener. With beginner Portuguese, record in English.

## 5. Brazilian professional culture: relationships, WhatsApp, coffee, formality, indicação

### Takeaway
Brazilian business trust is relationship-based (affective before cognitive). People move to first names quickly, and WhatsApp is the default channel once you have met: exchanging numbers matters more than business cards, and a same-day follow-up is normal. São Paulo meetings are punctual and decisions are slow and hierarchical. Indicação (referral, "QI = quem indica") is a primary hiring channel. LinkedIn is very large in Brazil (about 100M users, June 2026) and is the place for first contact with people you have not met.

### Cited Findings
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, secondary account of Erin Meyer's *The Culture Map*] Brazil leans relationship-based on Meyer's "Trusting" scale. Personal connection, for example a long lunch about family, can matter more than punctuality and follow-through. — [NAR article on Meyer](https://www.nar.realtor/articles/where-do-you-and-your-clients-fit-on-the-culture-map); caveat that "Latin cultures are relationship-oriented" is too simplistic — [George Fox DLGP blog](https://blogs.georgefox.edu/dlgp/cross-cultural-dynamics/)
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, 7 Jun 2026] WhatsApp "dominates Brazilian professional communication". Swapping WhatsApp numbers at events is more meaningful than swapping cards. A follow-up within 24 hours, ideally the same evening, is the norm (for example, "Ótimo te conhecer hoje! Vamos marcar aquele café?"). Brazilians move to first names quickly, and "senhor/senhora" with a peer-aged professional sounds stiff. LinkedIn is for formal records and for people not yet met. — [Kaiwa, Portuguese networking phrases 2026](https://trykaiwa.com/blog/portuguese-networking-event-phrases-2026)
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, 2024–2026 commercial etiquette guides] Relationships and trust come first. Meetings open with small talk, and rushing to business can seem rude. Don't expect decisions in a first meeting. Senior leaders approve decisions even in informal teams. Punctuality matters more for São Paulo business meetings. Avoid politics and religion. — [ClickUp Brazil etiquette](https://clickup.com/p/business-etiquette/brazil); [Healy Consultants](https://blog.healyconsultants.com/business-etiquette-in-brazil/); [Pebl](https://hellopebl.com/resources/blog/business-etiquette-in-brazil)
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL] About 169M Brazilians use WhatsApp, and it is widely used at work between employees and clients. — [NSC Total](https://www.nsctotal.com.br/?p=6851762). WhatsApp is on the home screen of 64.8% of Brazilian smartphones (Super Panorama Mobile Time/Opinion Box; publication date not shown) — [Mobile Time](https://www.mobiletime.com.br/?p=855811). In a June 2025 Opinion Box study, 97% of 1,126 respondents opened WhatsApp at least daily — [Canaltech](https://canaltech.com.br/apps/o-que-os-brasileiros-mais-fazem-no-whatsapp-segundo-pesquisa/) (**TO_VERIFY** exact attribution).
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL] LinkedIn reached 100M users in Brazil (Meio & Mensagem, 10 Jun 2026) — [Meio & Mensagem](https://www.meioemensagem.com.br/midia/como-o-linkedin-se-consolidou-no-mercado-brasileiro). - [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL, Oct 2025; regional] A CDL-BH survey found 53% of commerce and services owners in Belo Horizonte name referral by employees or acquaintances ("indicação", "QI") as their main hiring channel. — [Hoje em Dia](https://www.hojeemdia.com.br/empregoecarreiras/qi-quem-indica-e-decisivo-na-contratac-o-em-mais-de-50-das-vagas-no-comercio-de-bh-diz-pesquisa-1.1090461). A Robert Half figure (Brazil 2nd worldwide in "QI" use) is undated — [O Popular](https://opopular.com.br/noticias/economia/quem-indica-%C3%A9-tend%C3%AAncia-no-mercado-1.929579) (**TO_VERIFY**).

### Inferences
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Channel ladder:** LinkedIn or community DM for first contact, then a call or coffee, then WhatsApp once a number is offered. Never cold-WhatsApp a number you were not given; it is also an LGPD issue (§6). "Vamos marcar um café?" is the natural ask. Propose a specific place near their office (Faria Lima, Itaim, Pinheiros, Vila Olímpia) and two time slots.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Warmth with structure:** a friendly first-name tone and a little personal context (why São Paulo, learning Portuguese) build rapport. In São Paulo, be on time and keep the meeting crisp. Expect slow, hierarchy-gated decisions, so keep relationships warm with occasional value-adding touches (an article, congratulations on a launch), not chasing.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Indicação etiquette:** in Brazil a referral puts the referrer's own reputation at stake. Earn it with at least one real conversation, make it low-effort (a forwardable blurb), and close the loop with a thank-you whatever the outcome. Learning even basic Portuguese is a visible relationship signal: a few phrases in person, English for substance.

## 6. Limits: LinkedIn rules (invitations, InMail, automation), LGPD, reputational risk

### Takeaway
LinkedIn does not publish a weekly invitation cap (third parties say about 100/week). Hitting it typically brings a one-week restriction that LinkedIn will not shorten. Free members can add personal notes to only 5 invitations a month. All automation, scraping and engagement bots break the User Agreement (§8.2) and LinkedIn enforces this, including 2025 lawsuits against scrapers. LGPD still applies to publicly accessible profile data (ANPD), and its personal-use exemption requires purposes that are both private and non-economic. Job-search contact lists should therefore be minimal, manually built and purpose-limited. Mass outreach also gets people removed from communities such as Locally Optimistic.

### Cited Findings
- [VERIFIED_OFFICIAL, page "last updated 2 years ago", © 2026] LinkedIn prohibits third-party crawlers, bots, browser plug-ins or extensions that scrape, modify or automate activity. This covers automated methods that add or download contacts, send or redirect messages, or like, comment on or share posts. Also prohibited: bypassing use limits, fake engagement and algorithm manipulation. These are tied to User Agreement §8.2, with a risk of account restriction or shutdown. — [LinkedIn Help: Prohibited software and extensions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387)
- [VERIFIED_OFFICIAL, accessed 2026-10-15] Reaching the invitation limit can restrict sending, "typically one week". Withdrawing pending invitations does not lift it, invitations cannot be bought, and LinkedIn cannot shorten the wait. "Basic and free LinkedIn members can include a personalized message to five connection requests per month", while Premium members get unlimited personalized notes. LinkedIn recommends inviting only "people you know and trust". — [LinkedIn Help: Invitation limit reached](https://www.linkedin.com/help/billing/answer/a550555)
- [PRACTICAL_EXTERNAL / TO_VERIFY, conflicting] The weekly cap is unpublished; most 2025–2026 vendor sources say about 100/week on a rolling 7-day window. Mature or Sales Navigator accounts reportedly get 150–200 and new accounts about 80. An older 2022 source said about 200. Pending-invitation thresholds conflict: about 1,500 vs 3,000–5,000. — [Topo](https://www.topo.io/blog/linkedin-invitation-limit-guide); [Findymail](https://www.findymail.com/blog/linkedin-connection-request-limit/); [Contentin glossary](https://contentin.io/glossary/weekly-invitation-limit/); [Waalaxy (2022)](https://blog.waalaxy.com/?p=23653)

### Inferences
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Safe operating envelope:** manual actions only, with no extensions or automations (including "AI agent" or MCP tools that click or send on LinkedIn). Send about 10–20 invitations a week, almost all to people with shared context. Keep pending invitations low by withdrawing stale ones after about 3 weeks. If you want notes on more than 5 invites a month, take Premium Career during active sprints, or send a bare invite and message after acceptance.
- [HYPOTHESIS, not legal advice] **LGPD posture:** job seeking arguably has an economic purpose, so do not rely on the art. 4 I personal-use exemption; act as if the LGPD applies. Collect only what you need (name, company, role, public profile URL, where you met, date, status, next action). Use it only for this networking purpose. Do not scrape, buy lists, or run email-finder or enrichment tools on Brazilian contacts. Do not add contacts to newsletters. Never record sensitive data (health, religion, politics, ethnicity). Delete on request. Use only email addresses a person has published or given you.
- [HYPOTHESIS] **Reputational risks:** template-looking messages, multiple people messaged simultaneously at the same small team (they compare notes), asking for referrals from strangers, chasing more than twice, and pitching in community DMs. All of these travel fast in São Paulo's relatively small data-leadership circle and in the Locally Optimistic and Lenny's Slacks.

- Brazil's e-mail marketing self-regulation code (CAPEM) and its applicability to one-to-one networking emails were not researched.
